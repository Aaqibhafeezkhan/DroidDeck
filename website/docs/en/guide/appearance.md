# Appearance

The phone app has its own look settings, under **⚙️ Settings → Appearance**. Changes apply right away and
are saved on the phone, so each phone can have a different look.

![Deck with the Neon theme](../../assets/screenshots/deck-neon.png)

## Themes

A theme sets the background, colours and button style of the whole app in one tap:

| Theme | Look |
|---|---|
| **Classic** | Dark grey with blue, solid buttons (the original look). |
| **Neon** | Navy blue, double frame with a cut corner. |
| **OLED** | Pure black with outlined buttons. Saves battery on OLED screens. |
| **Amber** | Warm dark tones with amber, solid buttons. |
| **Synthwave** | Deep purple with magenta, double frame. |

![Appearance screen: themes, accent colour and button style](../../assets/screenshots/appearance-themes.png)

On top of the theme you can change:

- **Mode** — *System* follows the phone's light/dark setting; *Light* and *Dark* force
  one. The themes above describe the dark look; the light one is derived from the accent
  colour.
- **Accent color** — the colour of switches, links and highlights. *From theme* keeps
  the theme's own.
- **Button style** — *Solid* (filled with the button's colour), *Outline* (border
  in the button's colour) or *Frame* (double frame with a coloured corner). *From theme* keeps the theme's
  own.

In the *Outline* and *Frame* styles the colour you pick for a button in the configurator becomes the
colour of its icon and corner instead of its fill. Neutral colours, like the editor's default grey, stay
white.

![Deck with the OLED theme and amber accent](../../assets/screenshots/deck-oled.png)

## Language

**Language** picks the app's language: **Automatic** (the phone's), **Português**, **English** or **Español**. Action
types and messages from the PC follow the same language.

## Follow the focused app

**Follow the focused app** lets this phone switch profiles on its own when a
program with its own profile gets focus on the PC (see [Profiles](profiles.md#auto-switch)). On by default.

## Vibration

**Vibrate on tap** gives a short buzz when you touch a button, like a physical key:
*Off*, *Light*, *Medium* or *Strong*. When a timer ends the phone
gives a strong buzz, unless vibration is off.

## Deck only

**Deck only** hides the audio tab: the app opens straight into the deck, in full screen, with no tabs
at the bottom. Handy for a phone that sits on the desk as a dedicated deck.

![Live preview and the Deck only switch](../../assets/screenshots/appearance-top.png)

To reach the settings again, tap the full-screen button in the bottom-right corner: the top bar comes
back with the **⚙️** button.

**Reset** in the top-right corner of the Appearance screen goes back to the default theme.
